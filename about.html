04 · Leadership

The people
behind the group.

“A thousand-mile journey begins with a single step.”

The story of El Alfy began in 1985 with the acquisition of its first quarry. It has since grown into a group working in natural stone, contracting and manufacturing.

Over the years we added woodworking, glass, aluminium and decorative steel, in seven facilities built around strict quality control.

The Saraya facilities opened a new chapter, so we can carry development projects in-house from start to finish. Manufacturing remains at the heart of what we do.

Our commitment is unchanged: to work with our clients from the first stages of design, and to build relationships that outlast every project.

05 · Group structure

One house,
two brands.

El Alfy Stone sells the material. El Alfy Fit Out hands over the finished interior. The holding endorses both, and signs for the divisions that belong to neither.

The interior: refurbishment, renovation and complete interiors, designed, made in-house and installed under one contract.

The material: blocks, slabs, bundles, cut-to-size and the sample board, for designers, contractors and buyers.

DivisionManufacturing

Stone, wood, glass, aluminium and steel, made for Fit Out and Stone.

DivisionFF&E

Studio Interna and Rocknest, our product design brands.

El Alfy HoldingMass production

Wood doors in volume, supply only.

built for scale.
